Seoyoung Ju

I am currently pursuing an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. program in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Korea University, where I am advised by Prof. Taui-Kam in the MAI Lab. I received my bachelor’s degree in Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence from Sangmyung University.

My academic and professional experience includes serving as a Research Engineer Intern at Gnewsoft, alongside concurrent research internships at the MAI Lab and IMSI Lab, under the supervision of Prof. Taui-Kam and Prof. Nam-joon Kim, respectively. My research interests focus on Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Medical AI, and Generative Modeling.
